## Why it is here

Home Assistant identifies each integration by an icon for the device or service
it talks to — that is the purpose of the
[home-assistant/brands](https://github.com/home-assistant/brands) repository,
and since Home Assistant 2026.3 a custom integration may ship the same assets
locally and have them served through HA's brands proxy.
